  EmeraldCorps  

The Emerald Corps Job Application Game is an interactive web art experience created by Jon-Jon Rodriguez, he created it as an inspiration from Neil.Fun's website webgames such as the "Password Game," and the "I am not a robot game." Jon-Jon created this piece to highlight the kind of expectations that job companies expect for their future employees, creating a mock application mixed with absurd levels and challenges.
